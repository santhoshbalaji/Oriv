Clarity begins with less

A calm interface gives every element a reason to be there. It lets the page lead, while navigation stays familiar and close at hand. The best details are often the ones we stop noticing.

Space is more than an absence of content. It gives an idea time to settle, separates one thought from the next, and helps us find our place without having to search.

Let the content be the destination. Let the interface be the way.

A material with a purpose

Translucent controls can preserve a sense of context as a page moves underneath them. Used with restraint, Liquid Glass makes the relationship between the page and its navigation unmistakable.
