Privacy Policy

Oriv doesn’t collect your data.

This policy covers the Oriv browser for Mac and iPhone and this website, oriv.dev. Last updated 27 November 2026.

The short version

  • Oriv has no analytics, telemetry, tracking or advertising.
  • The developer receives nothing about what you browse, search or save.
  • Your tabs, bookmarks, history and settings stay on your devices, plus in your own iCloud account if you turn on sync.
  • Oriv has no accounts and no servers of its own that receive your browsing.

What stays on your device

Oriv stores your tabs, bookmarks, history, profile settings and website data (such as cookies) locally, separately for each profile. Private tabs and private windows keep their website data in memory and discard it when they close; they don’t add to history.

Address bar suggestions come from your own open tabs, bookmarks and history on the device. Oriv doesn’t use a remote suggestion service.

What leaves your device, and where it goes

Websites you visit

Like any browser, Oriv connects to the websites you open. Those sites see your IP address and the requests your browser makes, under their own privacy policies. Oriv’s content blocking reduces third-party trackers and ads, but it can’t hide your IP address.

Your search engine

When you submit a search, Oriv sends it to the search engine you chose in Settings. Nothing is sent while you type.

Site icons

To show a site’s icon, Oriv requests it from that same site, without cookies. Oriv doesn’t use a third-party icon service.

Optional iCloud sync

If you turn on sync, Oriv stores your tabs, bookmarks, history and profile settings in your private iCloud database, using Apple’s CloudKit, so your other devices can read them. Website logins, cookies and passwords never sync. Apple handles this data under Apple’s privacy policy; the developer can’t read it.

Passwords

Oriv fills passwords through Apple Passwords AutoFill. macOS and iOS handle your saved passwords, and Oriv never sees, stores or sends them.

Blocking lists

Oriv’s ad, tracker and cookie-banner rules are bundled with the app and update only when you update Oriv. The app doesn’t download lists in the background.

Crash reports

Oriv doesn’t include a crash reporter. If you choose to share analytics with app developers in your Apple device settings, Apple may share crash reports with the developer, under Apple’s terms. You can turn this off in System Settings or the Settings app at any time.

This website

oriv.dev has no cookies, analytics or third-party scripts. It’s hosted on GitHub Pages, so GitHub may log technical information such as your IP address to operate and secure the service; see GitHub’s privacy statement. Links to GitHub take you to github.com under GitHub’s own policies.

Children

Oriv doesn’t knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children, because it doesn’t collect personal information at all.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will appear on this page with a new date, and its history is public in the Oriv repository. Oriv is open source, so you can read exactly what the app does. For questions, open an issue on GitHub.
