Support

How can we help?

Oriv is open source and developed in the open. Questions, bug reports and ideas all go through GitHub.

Report a bug or request a feature

Open an issue on GitHub Issues. Include your Oriv version, your macOS or iOS version, and the steps that cause the problem. Please leave out personal information such as passwords or private links.

Requirements

  • Mac: macOS 26 or later, on Apple silicon or Intel.
  • iPhone: iOS 26 or later. Coming soon through a TestFlight beta, then the App Store.

Common questions

A website looks broken

Content blocking can occasionally break a page. Open Site Privacy from the page menu and choose Site broken? Turn protections off for this site. The exception applies only to that site, and Reset Site Protections undoes it.

How do I fill a saved password?

Oriv uses Apple Passwords AutoFill. On Mac, click the key in the login field or in the toolbar, or press ⌥⇧⌘P, then choose your account. On iPhone, tap the key above the keyboard. Make sure AutoFill is turned on in your device’s Passwords settings.

What does sync share?

Sync is off until you turn it on. It shares tabs, bookmarks, history and profile settings through your own iCloud account. Website logins, cookies and passwords never sync. See the privacy policy.

How do I switch profiles?

On Mac, swipe with two fingers on the sidebar, or press ⌥⌘← and ⌥⌘→. Each profile keeps its own tabs, bookmarks, history, logins and colour.
